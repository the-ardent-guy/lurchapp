# LURCH — Complete Project Brief
*Last updated: April 2026*

---

## What Is Lurch

A satirical dating app website that looks completely real on the surface — polished, VC-funded, buzzword-heavy — but every single thing it says is quietly indicting the culture it's pretending to serve. The joke lands hardest when the satire is deadpan. No winking. Full sincerity. The reader has to sit with the discomfort of recognising themselves.

**The thesis underneath the satire:**
> Intimacy is not found. It is built — slowly, with someone imperfect, in a context where you both show up repeatedly, in a body that is physically present, doing something that matters. The app is the opposite of all of that.

**This is a fun/passion project. Not a commercial priority. Keep execution light and low-maintenance.**

## The Name

**LURCH** — the feeling. The involuntary forward motion you didn't choose. Being pulled into something before you were ready.

## Brand Identity

### Aesthetic Direction
**Bruised Romanticism.** The aesthetic of a dating app that got its heart broken, went to therapy, and came back with better taste.

### Colours
- Oxblood red
- Dirty cream / off-white
- Ink black
- Acid yellow (accent — for when the satire bites hardest)
- No pink. No gradients. No soft purples.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Heavy condensed serif — old editorial bones, magazine that's been printing since 1954 and just discovered irony
- **Satirical fine print:** Typewriter-adjacent mono
- **Body:** Clean sans
- **Logo:** LURCH — all caps, heavy condensed serif, 2° tilt, slightly off-kilter. Like it's mid-stumble. No icon. No heart. No flame.

### Texture
Newsprint grain. Slightly worn. Like the app was printed, crumpled, smoothed back out.

### Imagery
No stock photography of happy couples. Instead: empty restaurant tables, hands reaching for phones, two people facing away, a half-eaten dinner. Documentary. Unglamorous. True.

## /truth — The Three Exits

When someone clicks "Find My Person" or "Sign Up for Early Access" they land here.

**Header:** *"You clicked the button. That took something."*

Three honest exits:

### 1. Events — Lurch Out
- Curated IRL romantic date experiences: pottery, supper clubs, night hikes, bookbinding, literary salons
- **Platform:** Luma (free, embeddable, events auto-expire when date passes)
- **Model:** Rish is curator, not organiser. Find cool shit, add to Luma, it appears on site, disappears after.
- External events = manually curated cards with title, date, city, "Find Out More" link
- **Copy:** *"No algorithm. No profile. No AI wingman. Just you, a room full of people, and something worth doing."*

### 2. Therapists — Signpost Only
**No direct connection. Liability risk.** Just links to vetted platforms:
- iCall (India-based, affordable)
- Vandrevala Foundation (24/7, India)
- BetterHelp / Wysa (app-based, low friction)
- The Mind Clan (Indian therapist directory)
- Practo (mental health section)

**Copy:** *"We're not equipped to help you with this. These people are."*

### 3. Blog / Reads
Links to myth-busting articles. 20 planned. See full list in Notion.

## Key Research Threads — What They All Say

All four thinkers land in roughly the same place:

| The Problem | The False Solution | The Real Solution |
|---|---|---|
| You're lonely | Find the right person | Build community and belonging first |
| The spark died | Find someone new | Tend to desire deliberately |
| You keep attracting wrong people | Better filters | Do the inner work |
| Relationships feel hard | Find an easier one | Learn repair, learn to fight well |
| You feel unknown | More dates | Let someone actually know you |

## Identified Flaws — Keep In Mind

1. **Tonal whiplash risk** — satire to therapist to pottery class is a hard journey. Transitions must be handled carefully or it feels like three websites stapled together.
2. **City problem with events** — Bengaluru-first vs global. Events section breaks if user isn't local.
3. **One-visit website risk** — blog and events solve return visits, but only if maintained.
4. **Blog is the hardest part** — most valuable and shareable thing here. Needs consistent writing. Two posts and abandoned = loses all credibility.
5. **No clear single primary audience** — satire works for everyone but the exits assume different needs.
6. **Message/medium tension** — a website telling people to go offline. Zine idea floated as future offline extension. Most powerful version of Lurch might not live only on a screen.
