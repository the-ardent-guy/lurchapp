## THE SCIENCE

Keep. 3–4 stats only. Big numbers, tight copy. No paragraph explanation around them — just the stat and one brutal line.

**Stat 1**
> **26% lonelier**
> Heavy dating app users score 26% higher on validated loneliness scales. The product built to cure loneliness is measurably worsening it.

**Stat 2**
> **0.78 dates / month**
> 140 profiles judged per day. 35 matches. 0.78 dates. The conversion rate of a spam newsletter. With more emotional consequences.

**Stat 3**
> **78% ghosted**
> 78% of users report being ghosted. 65% admit to ghosting someone. Nobody signed up to become this.

**Stat 4**
> **37% lower self-worth**
> Tinder users reported 37% lower self-esteem than non-users. The app monetising your loneliness is also monetising your insecurity.

## DESIGN NOTES

**Typography:** LURCH all caps, heavy condensed serif, 2° tilt on hero headline. Keep existing system.

**Palette:** Oxblood red, dirty cream, ink black, acid yellow accent. Newsprint grain texture. No pink. No gradients.

**Tone:** Deadpan corporate for the shock cards. Dry and authoritative for the stats. Direct and warm for the honest version exit.

**Pacing:** The page should feel short. Each section should land and get out. If you're scrolling for more than 3 seconds on a section, it's too long.

**Shock cards layout:** Consider making the Caste Filter card slightly larger or differently weighted than the others. It's the one people will screenshot.
